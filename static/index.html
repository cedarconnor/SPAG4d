Workflow

  1. Upload a 2:1 equirectangular panorama (JPG/PNG)
  2. Adjust parameters below (defaults work well for most scenes)
  3. Convert — runs depth estimation + SPAG conversion (~1-2s)
  4. Preview in the 3D viewer: left-click orbit, right-click pan, scroll zoom
  5. Refine (optional) — AI gap filling with Klein 9B synthesis
  6. Heatmap — toggle to see which splats are new vs original
  7. Download as PLY for Blender, SuperSplat, or any 3DGS viewer

Conversion Parameters

Depth Model
DA360 (default) = scale-invariant disparity with 360° padding. DAP = metric depth for panoramas.
SHARP (experimental)
Per-face neural refinement. Experimental — SPAG mode is faster and usually better.
Stride
Pixel stride for Gaussian generation. 1 = max quality, 2 = balanced (default), 4 = fast preview.
Depth Range
Valid depth range in meters. Points outside this are clipped.
Sky Cutoff
Max distance in meters. Points beyond this are removed. Use 30m for indoor scenes.
Floaters Cleanup
Outlier removal strength. 0 = off, higher = more aggressive.
Depth Scale
Global depth multiplier. Adjusts overall scene scale.

Splat Refinement

After conversion, use refinement to fill gaps and improve coverage. Klein 9B synthesizes novel views that seed new Gaussians in hole regions.

Camera Mode
Orbit = horizontal ring. Orbit + Above/Below = adds elevated cameras. Full Sphere = three elevation rings for max coverage. Custom = navigate the viewer, click "Add Camera" to place your own viewpoints.
Radius
Camera distance from center (meters). Larger radius detects more gaps via parallax.
Cameras
Number of viewpoints. More = better coverage, slower (~6 min per camera with Klein).
Rounds
Refinement iterations. 1 round is usually sufficient.
Heatmap Toggle
After refinement, click Heatmap to color-code splats by origin. Blue = original, Green = promoted (validated new), Yellow = seeded (candidate).
Quick Tips: Start with Orbit, 4 cameras, 1 round for a quick test. Use Full Sphere for scenes with visible floors/ceilings. After refinement, toggle Heatmap to verify new splats are in the right places.
